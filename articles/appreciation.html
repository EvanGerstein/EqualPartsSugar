How to Show Your Appreciation

Member circular • November 12, 2001 • restored copy

A little courtesy goes a long way. Our host asks for very little. When you return where you first met him, please remember these five small gestures, in order.

Look up when your host enters.

Look down when he takes his seat.

Look left to acknowledge your neighbor.

Look right to acknowledge the place you believe is empty.

Look down once more.

When a space is provided, write the two words he is waiting for: THANK YOU.

We took turns cooking. There was nobody to thank.

These instructions have always been included in your welcome pack.
